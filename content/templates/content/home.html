Who We Are

A movement centered on Jesus

Faith in God exists to help people know Jesus, grow as disciples and participate in God's mission. We believe discipleship is not simply a program, but a way of life that is passed from one generation to another.

01

Discipleship

Following Jesus means learning from Him and becoming more like Him. We seek to help people grow in faith, character and obedience.

02

Multiplication

Disciples make disciples. We want the life and teaching we receive to be passed on to others who can pass it on again.

03

Churches

Disciples gather into local fellowships where people worship Jesus, encourage one another and participate in His mission.
